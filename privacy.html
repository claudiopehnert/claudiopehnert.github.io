Privacy policy

notify-mcp is used solely by its operator for personal notifications.

  • The app uses the Gmail scope gmail.send only to send emails from the operator's own account to recipients configured by the operator.
  • The app does not read, store, or share any email content or other Google user data.
  • A minimal send log (timestamp, subject, status) is kept for operational purposes.
